# 🎵 Queueit

**Your party. Your music. Everyone in the queue.**

Queueit is an Android app that makes it easy for everyone at a party to add songs to a shared Spotify queue.

### How it works

One person connects their Spotify account and plays music through their speaker. Queueit generates a **QR code** that others can scan directly from the app.

Once connected, everyone can search for their favorite songs and add them to the Spotify queue — **from their own phone**.

No passing phones around. No fighting over the playlist. Just scan, pick a song, and queue it. 🎶

### 💡 The idea

Queueit is designed to make music at parties more **social and collaborative**, giving everyone a chance to contribute to the queue while keeping control of playback with the host.

Built with the **Spotify API**.
